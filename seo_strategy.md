# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages
- Service pages
- State and city service-area pages
- Resource hub and resource guide pages
- About, contact, privacy policy, terms of service, and HTML sitemap pages

## Out of scope
- Authenticated or internal admin routes under `/admin/**`
- API routes under `/api/**` except where they affect crawlability metadata

## Target audience
- Businesses in Idaho and Utah looking for commercial cleaning or construction cleanup services.

## Primary keywords
- commercial cleaning Idaho
- commercial cleaning Utah
- construction cleaning Idaho
- construction cleaning Utah
- city-level commercial cleaning and construction cleanup terms
