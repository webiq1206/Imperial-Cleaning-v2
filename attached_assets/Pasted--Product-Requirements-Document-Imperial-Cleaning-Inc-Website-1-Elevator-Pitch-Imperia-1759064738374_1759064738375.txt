# Product Requirements Document: Imperial Cleaning Inc. Website  

## 1. Elevator Pitch  
Imperial Cleaning Inc., based in Idaho Falls, ID, needs a professional business website optimized for SEO to generate inbound leads from organic search. The site will position the company as the go-to provider for construction cleaning and commercial cleaning services across Idaho and Utah. The design must replicate the style and layout of **Resend.com (see attached screenshot for reference)** with Imperial Cleaning’s branding, logos, and service content.  

## 2. Who is this app for  
- **Primary audience:** Commercial contractors seeking post-construction cleaning services.  
- **Secondary audience:** Commercial businesses in Idaho and Utah needing ongoing professional cleaning services.  

## 3. Functional Requirements  

### Core Features  
- **SEO Optimization**  
  - Title tags, meta descriptions, H1/H2/H3 structure, keyword targeting.  
  - Schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, OpeningHours, multiple service areas).  
  - XML sitemap + robots.txt.  
  - Fast load speed with compressed images and minified CSS/JS.  
  - Mobile-first responsive design.  

- **Service Pages**  
  - Construction Cleaning (primary service, optimized for “construction cleaning Idaho” and “construction cleaning Utah”).  
  - Commercial Cleaning (optimized for “commercial cleaning Idaho” and “commercial cleaning Utah”).  
  - Optional placeholder pages for future (About, Service Area, Blog, Careers).  

- **Service Area Pages**  
  - Dedicated “Service Areas” section highlighting coverage in Idaho and Utah.  
  - Content should include city/regional keywords for SEO.  
  - Internal linking between service pages and service area pages for SEO reinforcement.  

- **Contact & Conversion**  
  - Sticky contact options on all devices:  
    - **Click-to-call button** (tel:+12085203202).  
    - **Click-to-text button** (sms:+12085203202).  
    - **Click-to-email button** (mailto:service@imperialcleaninginc.com).  
  - **Survey-Style Multi-Step Contact Form**  
    - Progress bar at top showing completion status.  
    - One question per step: Name, Company, Email, Phone, Service Needed, Location, Message/Project Details.  
    - “Next” and “Back” navigation buttons.  
    - Confirmation screen on completion.  
    - Submissions mapped into HubSpot CRM.  
  - Inline “Request a Quote” CTAs across all pages.  

- **Analytics & Tracking**  
  - Google Analytics 4 integration.  
  - Google Search Console integration.  
  - HubSpot form integration with lead source tracking.  

- **Accessibility & UX**  
  - WCAG AA-compliant contrast ratios.  
  - Large, high-contrast CTAs.  
  - Mobile-friendly sticky navigation.  

### Hero Section (Hero Logo Integration)  
- The **Hero Logo (file attached)** must be central to the hero design.  
- Logo must appear as a **large transparent background element** across **all devices**.  
- The background logo should be layered under a **black overlay** for subtlety.  
- The logo must include a **“breathing” animation effect** (slow, smooth growth/shrink cycle).  
- Hero content (headline, subheadline, CTA) must remain sharp and readable on top:  
  - Headline: *“Trusted Commercial & Construction Cleaning Across Idaho & Utah”*  
  - Subheadline: *“Professional cleaning solutions built for your business.”*  
  - CTA: *“Request a Quote”*  

### Header Navigation (Header Navigation Logo Integration)  
- The **Header Navigation Logo (file attached)** must be used in the site’s top navigation bar.  
- It should be scaled for readability and clarity against the black background.  
- On scroll, logo should remain visible and crisp (sticky header if implemented).  
- This logo may also be reused in the footer for brand consistency.  

## 4. User Stories  
- As a commercial contractor, I want to see that Imperial Cleaning provides professional post-construction cleaning so I can hire them to prepare my project for handover.  
- As a commercial business, I want to see that Imperial Cleaning offers reliable ongoing cleaning services so I can ensure my facility is maintained.  
- As a visitor, I want clear “Call Now” or “Request a Quote” options always available so I can contact them without scrolling.  
- As a visitor, I want the contact form to feel simple and interactive.  
- As a visitor, I want to immediately recognize Imperial Cleaning’s brand identity through the Hero Logo in the background and the Header Navigation Logo in the header.  
- As an admin, I want all form submissions to feed into HubSpot.  
- As a business owner, I want the site to rank on Google for “construction cleaning Idaho” and “construction cleaning Utah.”  

## 5. User Interface  

### Overall Design Requirements  
- The website must **mirror the design shown in the attached Resend.com screenshot**.  
- Developers/designers must **reference this screenshot directly** for spacing, typography, background treatments, card/grid layouts, footer, and interactive elements.  
- Only content, branding, and logos are to be changed — not the design language.  

### Specific Design Elements to Replicate  
1. **Colors & Backgrounds**  
   - Black backgrounds with subtle gradients/shadows.  
   - White and gray text with high contrast.  
   - Accent highlights styled minimally as in Resend.  

2. **Typography**  
   - Same font style as Resend.com (modern sans-serif).  
   - Large bold headlines, clean minimal body text.  
   - Identical hierarchy to screenshot.  

3. **Spacing & Layout**  
   - Section order, padding, and whitespace identical to screenshot.  
   - Each block separated by generous margins.  

4. **Hero Section**  
   - Uses **Hero Logo** (transparent, animated breathing effect).  
   - Foreground text + CTA centered and layered above black overlay.  

5. **Header Navigation**  
   - Uses **Header Navigation Logo** in top-left position.  
   - Dark navigation bar matching screenshot.  
   - Sticky/scroll behavior should mirror Resend.com.  

6. **Section Blocks (SEO-Optimized Information)**  
   - Replace Resend’s developer/code examples with **SEO-optimized informational content** about construction cleaning and commercial cleaning.  
   - Each block should target key SEO phrases (e.g., “construction cleaning Idaho,” “commercial cleaning Utah”).  
   - Layout must stay identical to Resend: text alignment, spacing, and grid structure unchanged.  

7. **Iconography & Visual Language**  
   - Icons must **look and feel like Resend.com icons**: flat, outline-only, minimal, modern.  
   - Absolutely no generic clipart.  
   - Icons must use **animated neon gradient transitions** (like Resend) when hovered or revealed on scroll.  
   - Gradient hues should remain within Resend’s cool neon palette (blues, purples, greens).  
   - Example icons:  
     - Construction Cleaning: building outline, hard hat outline.  
     - Commercial Cleaning: office tower outline, sparkle/shine outline.  
     - Reliability: shield outline, calendar outline.  
     - Efficiency: arrow-in-motion outline, progress bar outline.  

8. **Buttons & CTAs**  
   - All buttons must **match Resend.com’s styling exactly**: rectangular, clean typography, smooth hover states.  
   - Primary CTAs (e.g., “Request a Quote”) must use the **animated neon border style** from Resend.  
   - Secondary CTAs (e.g., “Learn More”) may use solid fill styling.  
   - Hover states must replicate Resend’s glow/fade transitions precisely.  

9. **Footer**  
   - Multi-column layout identical to Resend.  
   - Dark background, light text.  
   - Include: Services, Service Areas, Contact, Hours, Legal/Privacy.  

10. **Animations & Interactivity**  
    - Hero logo breathing effect must be smooth and subtle.  
    - Section fade-ins and scroll reveals must use Resend’s easing/timing.  
    - **Icons must use the same gradient animation library/technique as Resend.**  
    - Buttons and CTAs must use identical hover/transition effects as Resend.  
    - No custom deviations — animations must match Resend’s polish exactly.
