# Audit: 48 city pages (template)

Single source: `app/[state]/[service]/[city]/page.tsx` + `components/pages/service-location-client.tsx`. All 48 location pages are generated by one `generateMetadata` function and one client component, so the findings apply uniformly unless noted.

Pages covered: 24 cities x 2 services. URLs are enumerated in `SEO-AUDIT-INVENTORY.md`.

## Findings

| Severity | Defect | Status |
|---|---|---|
| MEDIUM | OG `images[0].url` is the single sitewide `/og-image.png` for all 48 pages. No city-specific imagery means social shares look identical. | DOCUMENTED (asset generation out of scope) |
| MEDIUM | Twitter card lacks `images`. | DOCUMENTED (layout default fills in) |
| LOW | Title pattern `${city} ${serviceName} | Imperial Cleaning ${stateAbbr}` runs 45 to 65 chars depending on city; longest cities (Northern Utah / Cache Valley, Eastern Idaho Extended) exceed 65 chars. | DOCUMENTED |
| LOW | Description guard truncates at 157 chars with ellipsis. Acceptable but rare cases lose the call-to-action. | DOCUMENTED |
| LOW | `service-location-client.tsx` emits per-location LocalBusiness, Service, BreadcrumbList, and FAQPage JSON-LD (verified). | OK |

## What is already correct (per page)
- Unique title, description, keywords driven by city data
- Self-referencing canonical URL
- OpenGraph block with siteName, locale, image
- Twitter summary_large_image card
- Geo meta tags (`geo.region`, `geo.placename`, `geo.position`, `ICBM`) per city coordinates
- Per-city LocalBusiness, Service, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage JSON-LD
- Internal links: UP (parent service hub and state hub), ACROSS (sibling service), NEARBY (2 to 3 nearby cities), plus `RelatedContent` block
- 404 handling for invalid state/service/city combos via `notFound()`

## Per-city deviations
None functional. Differences are limited to the data-driven fields (name, county, coordinates, keywords) and the title length variance noted above.
