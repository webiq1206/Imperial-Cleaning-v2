# Google Business Profile Optimization Checklist

Owner action items for the Imperial Cleaning Inc GBP listing. These cannot be done from the website code - they require logging into [business.google.com](https://business.google.com).

Canonical NAP (use this exact format everywhere):

- **Name:** Imperial Cleaning Inc
- **Address:** 2653 Clarence Pl, Idaho Falls, ID 83402 (matches Google listing)
- **Phone:** (208) 520-3202
- **Website:** https://imperialcleaninginc.com

---

## 1. Core profile fields (do once, ~30 minutes)

- [ ] Business name is exactly "Imperial Cleaning Inc" - no keywords stuffed in (e.g. NOT "Imperial Cleaning Inc - Commercial Cleaning Idaho Falls"). Keyword-stuffed names violate GBP rules and risk suspension.
- [ ] Primary category: **Commercial cleaning service**
- [ ] Secondary categories: **Janitorial service**, **House cleaning service** only if actually offered - otherwise skip
- [ ] Confirm ZIP shows/geocodes to **83402** (this was inconsistent across citations; the website now uses 83402 everywhere)
- [ ] Service areas: add the cities you actually serve - Idaho Falls, Ammon, Pocatello, Chubbuck, Blackfoot, Rexburg, Rigby, Shelley, St. Anthony, Driggs, Twin Falls, Boise, Meridian, Nampa; Utah: Logan, Ogden, Salt Lake City, Provo (GBP caps at 20 areas)
- [ ] Hours: Mon-Fri 8:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sat 9:00 AM-1:00 PM, Sun closed (Google listing is canonical; website footer and schema now match)
- [ ] Website URL: `https://imperialcleaninginc.com` (no trailing slash, no www)
- [ ] Appointment URL: `https://imperialcleaninginc.com/contact`
- [ ] Business description (750 chars max): lead with what + where, e.g. "Imperial Cleaning Inc is a family-owned commercial and construction cleaning company headquartered in Idaho Falls, Idaho, serving businesses and contractors across Idaho and Utah since 2015. We provide post-construction cleanup (rough, final, and touch-up phases) and recurring janitorial services for offices, medical facilities, restaurants, schools, and dealerships. Fully insured and bonded. Free quotes within 24 hours."
- [ ] Opening date: 2015

## 2. Services section (do once, ~20 minutes)

Add each as a named service under the matching category. Mirror the website's service pages:

- [ ] Post-Construction Cleaning (rough / final / touch-up phases)
- [ ] Commercial Janitorial Services
- [ ] Office Cleaning
- [ ] Medical Office Cleaning
- [ ] Restaurant Cleaning
- [ ] School & Daycare Cleaning
- [ ] Car Dealership Cleaning
- [ ] Floor Care (strip, wax, burnish)
- [ ] Window Cleaning (interior/reachable)

Do not enter prices in GBP services unless you are confident in them; "Get quote" is fine.

## 3. Photos (ongoing, the single biggest GBP ranking lever you control)

- [ ] Upload logo + cover photo
- [ ] Add 10+ real job photos to start: crews on site, before/after of post-construction cleans, equipment, branded vehicles if any
- [ ] Then add 2-4 new photos **every month** - recency matters
- [ ] Name files descriptively before upload (e.g. `post-construction-cleaning-idaho-falls.jpg`)
- [ ] No stock photos - Google detects and may remove them; they also undermine trust

## 4. Google Posts (15 minutes, weekly or biweekly)

- [ ] Post a project highlight, seasonal offer, or tip every 1-2 weeks
- [ ] Always include a photo and a CTA button linking to /contact
- [ ] Reuse website content: each new guide on /resources is a ready-made post

## 5. Q&A section (do once, then monitor)

- [ ] Seed 5-8 questions yourself (this is allowed) using the FAQs now on the website: pricing, service areas, scheduling, insurance, free quotes
- [ ] Answer from the business account
- [ ] Check monthly for new public questions - anyone can answer, so unanswered questions get filled by strangers

## 6. Reviews (see review-growth-playbook.md)

- [ ] Reply to **every** review within 48 hours, positive or negative
- [ ] In replies, naturally mention the service and city ("Thanks for trusting us with your post-construction clean in Rexburg!") - review reply text is crawled

## 7. Monthly maintenance (10 minutes)

- [ ] Check GBP Insights: search queries, calls, direction requests - note which cities/services drive calls
- [ ] Verify no unauthorized edits to categories/hours (Google applies "suggested edits" silently)
- [ ] Add new photos
- [ ] Publish at least one post
