**Ordered by commercial value, not by site structure.** The old queue ran
top-down through the site; this one runs money pages first and city pages last,
because crawl budget is the binding constraint and the 48 city pages have never
earned a fetch. Spending a scarce daily quota on `/idaho/commercial-cleaning/driggs`

the alphabet: "commercial janitorial services" (169 impressions), "commercial

zero clicks, so the pages answering them go first.

## How to work this list

1. Open [Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) with the
   `imperialcleaninginc.com` property selected.
2. Paste a URL into the inspection bar.
3. Wait for the result, then click **Request Indexing**.
4. Tick it off and move on.

Google's daily quota is roughly ten URLs. On "Quota exceeded", stop and resume
tomorrow — further requests that day will not register.

Requesting is not a guarantee. It puts the URL in a priority queue; indexing
still typically lands in days rather than instantly, and a page can be crawled
and still not indexed. Re-request anything still missing after two weeks.
