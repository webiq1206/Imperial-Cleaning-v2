# LinkedIn and Video Cadence

Playbook Sections 15 and 16. B2B proof and process content for facility managers, GCs, and property managers. Disclosure required. No fake endorsements.

## LinkedIn: 3 posts per week

Publish Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday (or Mon/Wed/Fri). Company page amplifies posts from leadership when possible.

### Post type rotation

| Week slot | Post type | Playbook opening to adapt |
|-----------|-----------|---------------------------|
| Post 1 | Commercial definition / local authority | "Commercial cleaning in Idaho Falls should be measured by what gets inspected, not what gets promised." |
| Post 2 | Construction BOFU or vendor-switch | "Post-construction cleaning is the last trade before the owner judges the whole project." OR "Switching janitorial companies usually happens after months of small misses, not one dramatic failure." |
| Post 3 | Medical, pricing, QA, or case study | "Medical office cleaning is not standard office cleaning with stronger chemicals." OR "Commercial cleaning cost per square foot only makes sense after frequency, floor type and scope are defined." |

Customize openings with a specific Idaho or Utah detail. Do not copy the same paragraph every week.

### Post structure (short form)

1. Opening line (pattern interrupt, no hype)
2. 3-5 sentences of operational insight
3. One proof point (photo, checklist, review quote with permission, or process detail)
4. Soft CTA: link to relevant money page or resource
5. Hashtags sparingly: `#CommercialCleaning` `#IdahoFalls` `#Janitorial` `#PostConstruction` (2-4 max)

### Disclosure rules

- If you are the owner or employee posting, state affiliation when recommending Imperial in a comment thread.
- Do not use fake testimonial quotes or stock photos labeled as your crews.
- Do not tag clients without written permission.
- Do not claim "preferred provider" or "#1" without verifiable source.

### Keyword-first indexing test (monthly)

1. Publish one post with a distinctive first sentence (example: "A cleaning checklist does not create accountability unless someone verifies the checklist.").
2. Wait 7-14 days. Search that exact sentence on Google in quotes.
3. Record whether the LinkedIn post URL appears. Log in [ai-prompt-tracker.md](../growth-playbook/ai-prompt-tracker.md) notes or scorecard.
4. If not indexed, LinkedIn is still a trust channel; do not over-invest in SEO-only tactics on LinkedIn alone.

## Weekly video series

One 90-180 second vertical video per week minimum. Clean master file without platform watermark. Post natively to YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram Reels.

### Series names and keyword-first titles

| Series | Setup | Example video title (keyword first) |
|--------|-------|-------------------------------------|
| Before the doors open | Night crew finishes before staff arrive | `Office Cleaning Idaho Falls: What Customers See Before Staff Arrive` |
| Final clean countdown | Construction approaching walkthrough | `Post-Construction Cleaning Idaho Falls: Final Clean Before Walkthrough` |
| What inspectors notice | Common misses in restrooms, glass, corners | `Commercial Cleaning Inspection: Spots Crews Miss on Final Walkthrough` |
| Floor rescue | Before/after floor care | `Commercial Floor Care Idaho Falls: When the Finish Is Failing, Not the Floor` |
| Facility manager audit | Walk-through with checklist | `Commercial Cleaning Checklist: Five Things Before Sign-Off` |
| Medical detail | High-touch clinical surfaces | `Medical Office Cleaning Idaho: Why the Scope Differs From Office Cleaning` |
| Showroom standard | Dealership glass, floors, lounges | `Dealership Cleaning Utah: Showroom Ready Before Sales Staff Arrive` |

Rotate series. Recycle top performers with new market or facility example after 8-12 weeks.

### Video production minimum

- Real site or facility b-roll (permission required on active jobs)
- Voiceover or on-camera from owner/ops lead (builds E-E-A-T)
- Captions burned in or SRT uploaded
- End card: phone + imperialcleaninginc.com/contact (soft CTA)
- Description: 2-3 sentences + link to matching money page

### Hook examples (first 3 seconds)

- "Your customers never see the cleaning crew. They see the result."
- "The project is finished. Now remove every sign that construction happened."
- "This is the spot a rushed cleaning crew leaves behind."
- "A showroom sells before the salesperson says a word."

## Opt-in engagement group (optional)

**Rules:**

- Never script fake endorsements or pay for comment pods.
- Violates platform policy and destroys trust if discovered.
