## Goal: first 100 target accounts

Quality over quantity. One hundred accounts you can research, personalize, and follow up with beats five hundred generic blasts.

### Segments to build (20-25 accounts each)

| Segment | Decision maker titles | Idaho / Utah facility examples (types, not clients) |
|---------|----------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| General contractors | Superintendent, project manager, construction manager | Commercial GC offices in Idaho Falls, Pocatello, Boise; healthcare and retail ground-up builders in Treasure Valley |
| Property managers | Regional PM, facilities director, building engineer | Multi-family and office portfolio managers along I-15 (IF to SLC corridor) |
| Medical / dental | Practice administrator, office manager, clinic director | Outpatient clinics, dental groups, urgent care in Bonneville and Bannock counties |
| Car dealerships | GM, office manager, facilities lead | Auto row and standalone dealerships in Eastern Idaho and Cache Valley |
| Schools / education | Facilities director, business manager | Charter schools, private academies, daycare centers in Rexburg, Idaho Falls, Logan |
| Manufacturers / warehouses | Plant manager, operations director | Food processing, logistics, and light industrial parks near Idaho Falls and Pocatello |

**Do not** paste fake company names into this doc or into emails. Build your list in a CRM spreadsheet with real research.

### Where to find accounts

- Active commercial permits and project lists (county/city portals)
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator or manual LinkedIn search by title + city
- BOMA, local chamber member directories, construction association member lists
- Google Maps category search (property management, general contractor) in target cities
- Referrals from existing happy clients (lowest-cost source)

## Email sequence (3 emails + close-the-loop)

Playbook Section 20. Keep first touch under 120 words. One observation, one proof point, one CTA.

### Email 1: Observation + proof + low-friction CTA

**Subject options (customize per account):**

- `{City} final clean before walkthrough`
- `Janitorial consistency across your {city} buildings`
- `Post-construction turnover on your {project type} schedule`

**Body template:**

```
Hi {First name},

I noticed {one specific observation: active project, new location, multi-site portfolio, visible facility type}.

Imperial Cleaning Inc is a commercial and construction cleaning company headquartered in Idaho Falls. We {one proof point: 3-phase construction cleanup / recurring janitorial with documented QA / medical office protocols}.

If {relevant pain: turnover date / missed cleans / inspection readiness} is on your radar, I can send a short scope outline or schedule a 15-minute call.

{Your name}
Imperial Cleaning Inc
(208) 520-3202
```

**Segment proof points (use only if true; confirm in proof ledger):**

| Segment | Proof asset to offer in email 2 |
|---------|--------------------------------|
| GC | Construction cleanup checklist + project quote path |
| Property manager | Inspection checklist + portfolio walkthrough |
| Medical | Medical office cleaning scope + facility assessment |
| Dealership | Showroom nightly cleaning checklist |
| School | School facility plan + background-checked crew note (confirm with owner) |
| Warehouse | Industrial scope example |

### Email 2 (3-4 business days later): Useful asset

**Subject:** `Checklist for {their facility type} cleaning scope`

Attach or link the matching resource from the website when live (`/resources/construction-cleanup-process-checklist`, industry page, etc.). No attachment of fabricated case studies.

```
Hi {First name},

Following up with a {checklist / scope outline} teams use before hiring a cleaning vendor for {facility type}.

If you want Imperial to walk your {site/building/project} and quote from actual scope, reply with a good time or call (208) 520-3202.

{Your name}
```

### Email 3 (4-5 business days after email 2): Proof example

Share one real photo, review quote, or anonymized project detail with permission. If no case study exists yet, use a process description instead of a fake client story.

```
Hi {First name},

One example of how we stage {rough/final/touch-up OR nightly janitorial} work: {2-3 sentences on process, no client name unless permitted}.

Worth a quick look at your {project/building}?

{Your name}
```

### Close-the-loop (5-7 business days after email 3)

**Subject:** `Should I close the loop?`

```
Hi {First name},

I have not heard back, so I will assume timing is not right. Should I close this out, or is there a better month to revisit {project/facility} cleaning?

Either way, the checklist link stays useful: {URL}

{Your name}
```

## Multi-channel follow-up (same week as email 1)

1. **Phone:** One call 1-2 days after email 1. Leave voicemail if no answer. Do not read the email verbatim.
2. **LinkedIn:** Connection request or InMail with a different angle (shorter, no PDF attachment).
3. **CRM:** Log every touch. Same script across channels gets ignored.

## Partner one-sheet outline

One PDF or printed page for GCs, property managers, and brokers. Use only verified facts from [proof-ledger.md](../growth-playbook/proof-ledger.md).

### Sections

1. **Headline:** Imperial Cleaning Inc | Commercial + Construction Cleaning | Idaho and Utah
2. **Coverage:** HQ Idaho Falls; service areas (24 cities); Tier 1 response markets
3. **Services:** Post-construction (rough, final, touch-up); janitorial; office; floor care; medical; dealership; school; industrial (only if operational)
4. **Why partners refer:** Single regional vendor, documented QA, quote within 24 hours (if confirmed), COI/W-9 ready
5. **Insurance:** General liability, workers' comp, bonded; additional insured and COI on request
6. **Quote process:** Call or web form > walkthrough > written quote
7. **Contact:** (208) 520-3202, service@imperialcleaninginc.com, imperialcleaninginc.com/contact
8. **Proof block:** Google rating link, one permissioned photo, one sentence from a real review (with attribution)

**Do not include:** Fake logos, unnamed "major clients," or preferred vendor claims without contract.

## Compliance

- CAN-SPAM: include physical address and opt-out on bulk email if using a mass tool.
- Do not misrepresent relationship or endorsements.
- Do not claim "preferred vendor" without a written agreement.
- Do not use purchased email lists.
