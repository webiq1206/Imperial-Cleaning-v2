# Executive Scorecard

Weekly and monthly KPI template aligned to playbook Section 23. A raw form fill is not a qualified lead. Track through to walkthrough, proposal, and won contract where possible.

**Owners:** Owner fills revenue and CRM fields. Marketing fills SEO, local, AI, and paid fields. Web supports landing-page conversion rates.

## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Qualified quote request | Form or call with service, location, facility type, and contact info; not spam or wrong service |
| Walkthrough | Scheduled on-site visit or virtual scope review |
| Proposal | Written quote or bid sent after walkthrough |
| Win | Signed contract or PO |
| Recurring MRR | Monthly contract value for janitorial/commercial recurring work |
| Project revenue | One-time construction or special project booking |
| CAC | Total marketing + sales acquisition cost / new customers in period |
| Recommendation share | Share of tracked AI prompts where Imperial is mentioned |
