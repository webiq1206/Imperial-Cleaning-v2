## Findings and priorities

The June 21 through September 20 construction-query slice included Idaho Falls searches with 59 to 84 impressions each, average positions around 4.6 to 8.2, and no recorded clicks. Those are relevant snippet and intent-match opportunities, but their small samples do not prove a particular cause. Broader construction-company and service terms averaged positions around 36 to 49, so snippets alone will not address their visibility.

A separate, partial May 21, 2025 through September 19, 2026 query/page export recorded 6 clicks from 6,864 impressions for commercial cleaning idaho falls on the homepage (0.0874% CTR). Commercial cleaning services idaho falls recorded 2 clicks from 1,995 impressions on the homepage. These historical observations justify retaining strong commercial landing pages while construction remains primary. They do not establish current cannibalization or today's CTR.

Confirmed presentation defects in the inspected implementation included automated mid-sentence description truncation on city pages, inconsistent brand wording across title/social fields, promotional claims not substantiated by this task, one generic social graphic, and only 16/32px icons in the declared favicon list. Google's current guidance recommends a favicon larger than 48px; the old icon was not inherently ineligible. Neither icon size nor metadata wording is proven to have caused the observed CTR.

Historical unrelated casino queries and old hostname variants occur in prior data. Do not mix them into the qualified-service opportunity denominator or infer a current compromise from historical impressions. Ads, map packs, AI answers, query mix, and competitors remain possible influences, not established causes in this blocked reporting environment.
